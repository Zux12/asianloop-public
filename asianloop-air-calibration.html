Walkthrough
WASD or arrow keys move. Drag to look. “Click to look around” locks your mouse; Esc releases it. On touch devices, move with the left pad and drag elsewhere to look.

Walls stay visible when walking. Equipment, furniture and walls have collision boundaries. Reset returns you to the entrance.
